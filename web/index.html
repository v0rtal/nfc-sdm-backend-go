Every tag has a unique identity and produces cryptographically verifiable messages, connecting each item to a record you can check. Those records help build provenance—the story of an item's origin and history. Chain of ownership is coming soon, so that story can follow an item as it changes hands.

TagTamper adds a layer of physical security: opening its tamper loop leaves a lasting record that the backend can verify, helping reveal interference with the tag's attachment or seal.

Nothing is perfect—but at least these tags have a better memory than the person who keeps losing their keys.
